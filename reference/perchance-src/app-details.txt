WHAT IT IS:
The application hub: boot, routing, playback UI, publishing.
TTS engine (engine.js) turns text into MP3 blobs locally; Player (player.js) streams them;
ServerNet (net.js) talks to Explore backend; share.js handles uploads + AI metadata;
views.js draws the three views. Nothing here uploads audio - published books are (text URL + voice).
